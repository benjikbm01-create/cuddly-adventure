# Roadmap

## v0.1 (this build)
- Starting region (~750 x 750) generated from source data; adventure-map protection; soft boundary
- 15 residents with daily schedules, rain routines, routine variants, ambient talk, tiered simulation
- Branching dialogue, quest engine, journal, case board, clue inspection
- Opening main-story segment, 3 side quests, 1 non-canonical sandbox test quest
- Stamina combat (light/heavy/dodge/guard/parry/counter/guard break), 2 enemy patterns, 2 encounters, checkpoints
- 2 abilities (movement technique, investigation instrument) with stamina/focus pools and cooldowns
- Category satchel, nine-slot hotbar with ability tokens, shops (buy/sell/restock), hearth cooking, gathering nodes
- Local Atlas map with discovered places, player and tracked lead
- Difficulty presets; client settings (HUD, tracker, markers, dialogue speed/size)

## Next (v0.2 and after)
- Hand-polish pass on the starting region (interiors, landmarks) with the commissioner's 32x textures
- Ability upgrade branches and respec; more techniques (grapple anchors, climbing, gliding)
- Companion basics, mounts, waystations
- Weather hazards, music by region/time/danger, reactive ambience
- Stealth (noise, sight, cover), witnesses and guards reacting to crime
- Festivals and recurring events; housing (optional)
- Manual save slots UI; more regions toward the 8,000 x 8,000 map
- Tier-2/Tier-3 crowd simulation for cities
- Continental Atlas with rumor vs. fact layers

Items listed under "Next" are NOT in v0.1.
