# The Hollow Atlas - public design summary (spoiler-free)

An original, nonlinear open-world fantasy action RPG built inside Minecraft Java 1.21.1.
You are an ordinary traveler. There is no prophecy and nothing you are obliged to own.

## Pillars
- **Cozy and serious at once:** markets, inns and gossip alongside difficult choices with lasting effects.
- **Investigation:** notice things, collect evidence, separate fact from rumor, form theories you can revise.
- **Living places:** residents keep homes, jobs, meals and friendships, and change routine when the village changes.
- **Hard, readable combat:** stamina, telegraphed enemies, dodges, guards, parries and counters. Difficulty presets from Story to Expert.
- **Mostly trained skills, rarely magic:** abilities are techniques and instruments; true wonders are rare.
- **A handcrafted world:** an authored map with real roads, landmarks and secrets. No random terrain, no digging.

## v0.1 slice
The first region of the western meadows: a market village with an inn, smithy, store, bakery, hall, mill, farms, an
orchard, woods, a hill overlook, a river, a cave, an old shrine and a closed road east. Fifteen named residents, an
opening story segment, three side quests, gathering, cooking, a merchant economy, two abilities and the first enemies.

## Systems the full game will grow from
Dialogue with conditions (evidence, reputation, relationships, time, weather, background), staged quests with
branching, a case board, data-driven residents with tiered simulation, flag-driven world changes, shops with restock,
station crafting, ability registry with upgrade branches, curated encounters, and a discovery-based Atlas.
